The NVKE controller is last to become Ready: it blocks on "no identity issued"
until SPIRE has issued its SVID, and its /readyz reports PostgreSQL and
Temporal, so it will not pass until both are up. A helm --wait that has already
returned does not mean it got there.

  kubectl -n {{ .Values.nvke.namespaces.controller }} rollout status deployment/nke-controller --timeout=10m
